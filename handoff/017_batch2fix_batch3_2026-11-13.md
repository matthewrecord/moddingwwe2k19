## Lessons from this session
- Check a creator's misc04 screenshot against the slot's **existing** row first: if they match, the mod targets that slot's stock parts (WM31 → 105).
- WrestleMINUS screenshots show misc01 small fields in hex and light colours as ARGB (tables store RGBA).
- New EPK8/EPAC ids load; new HSPC ids don't. EPK8 header word 8 = data size in bytes.
